Reliance Industries Limited — Annual Report 2023
Management Discussion and Analysis:
Revenue has grown by 20% to ₹80,000 Cr driven by retail and Jio segments. Risk Factors include market volatility, global supply chain disruptions, and regulatory changes in the telecom sector. The company reduced debt by 15% YoY maintaining a healthy balance sheet. Board approved a capex plan of ₹15,000 Cr for renewable energy expansion.
