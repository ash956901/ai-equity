# Indian Equity Analysis

## Overview

This skill provides domain knowledge for analysing companies listed on Indian exchanges (NSE and BSE). Use it whenever you need to evaluate financials, valuation multiples, or sector dynamics for Indian equities.

## Key Metrics & Conventions

### Currency & Units
- All figures in INR. Use Cr (crore = 10 million) for large numbers.
- Revenue, EBITDA, PAT are typically reported quarterly and annually.
- EPS is reported as face-value-adjusted; check face value (usually ₹1, ₹2, or ₹10).

### Must-check Ratios
| Ratio | What it tells you | Indian benchmark |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|
| ROE | Return on equity | >15% is good; >20% excellent |
| ROCE | Return on capital employed | >18% for capital-intensive sectors |
| Debt/Equity | Leverage | <1.0 for most sectors; <0.5 preferred for IT/FMCG |
| Current Ratio | Liquidity | >1.5 comfortable |
| Interest Coverage | Debt servicing ability | >3x healthy |
| Promoter Holding | Skin in the game | >50% is a positive signal |
| Promoter Pledge | Risk indicator | >20% pledged is a red flag |
| DII + FII holding | Institutional confidence | Rising trend is positive |

### Sector-specific Considerations
- **Banking/NBFC**: Focus on NIM, NPA ratios (GNPA, NNPA), CASA ratio, provision coverage, capital adequacy (CRAR).
- **IT Services**: Revenue growth in constant currency, deal wins (TCV), attrition rate, utilisation rate, margin trajectory.
- **Pharma**: ANDA pipeline, USFDA observations, API vs formulations mix, R&D spend %.
- **FMCG**: Volume growth vs value growth, rural vs urban mix, distribution reach.
- **Auto**: Monthly dispatches, EV transition progress, order book (for CVs).
- **Real Estate**: Pre-sales, collections, launch pipeline, net debt.

### Regulatory & Calendar
- Financial year: April–March (Q1=Apr-Jun, Q2=Jul-Sep, Q3=Oct-Dec, Q4=Jan-Mar).
- Results season: ~45 days after quarter end.
- SEBI regulations govern disclosures, insider trading, and corporate governance.
- RBI policies (repo rate, CRR, SLR) directly impact banking and rate-sensitive sectors.

## Analysis Framework

When performing a company analysis, follow this structure:

1. **Business Overview**: What does the company do? Market position, moat, competitive advantages.
2. **Financial Health**: Revenue trend (3-5 years), margin profile, cash flow generation.
3. **Valuation**: PE, PB, EV/EBITDA relative to sector peers and historical average.
4. **Growth Catalysts**: Expansion plans, new segments, market share gains.
5. **Risks**: Regulatory, concentration, commodity exposure, promoter governance.
6. **Shareholding Pattern**: Promoter changes, FII/DII trends, bulk/block deals.

## Red Flags to Watch

- Sudden spike in "other income" as % of PAT
- Consistent negative operating cash flow despite reported profits
- High contingent liabilities relative to net worth
- Frequent related-party transactions at non-arm's length
- Auditor qualifications or change of auditors
- Promoter holding reduction without disclosed reason
